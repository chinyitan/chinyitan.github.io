Chin Yi Tan’s Personal Website

Hi there, and welcome to my website! I’m Chin Yi Tan, a DiRAC Astrophysics Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Washington in Seattle (as of Oct 2026). I grew up in Puchong, Malaysia, completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Edinburgh, and earned my Ph.D. at the University of Chicago.

My research explores the structures surrounding our Milky Way, especially its satellite galaxies. I use these tiny galaxies to investigate the nature of dark matter, the mysterious substance that shapes galaxies and the growth of structure in the Universe.

If you have questions about my research or would like to get in touch, please email me at chinyi@uw.edu.
